Fig. X. Three-dimensional shapes of the Mab light-echo structure are consistent with a single common shape.

Each point is one independent sky window, fitted separately: the ellipsoidal second-order structure function of the reconstructed three-dimensional emissivity gives three principal axes $a_1 \ge a_2 \ge a_3$, plotted as the two independent axis ratios. Both ratios are lengths of the lag vector at fixed $S_2$, so they are independent of the structure-function slope. Error bars are $\pm1\sigma$ from block jackknife ($k = 4$); on a logarithmic axis a symmetric bar reaches the axis floor where the uncertainty exceeds the value. Windows are split at the quartiles of their echo signal-to-noise ratio (top S/N quartile 4.0–7.0; second quartile 2.3–3.7; lower half 1.4–2.3) and drawn back to front, so the best-measured quartile is never hidden. The grey diagonal is $a_2/a_1 = a_3/a_2$, separating prolate (cigar-like) from oblate (pancake-like) ellipsoids.

The blue diamond is the maximum-likelihood common shape of the highest signal-to-noise quartile, $a_2/a_1 = 0.28$, $a_3/a_2 = 0.60$. The dashed ellipse is the null hypothesis that every window has exactly this one shape and the observed spread is measurement error alone; its semi-axes are the median measurement uncertainties (0.248 and 0.073 dex) and it contains 5 of 29 windows in that quartile (17%) against the 39.4% a two-dimensional $1\sigma$ contour expects, so the null is rejected. The solid filled ellipse adds an intrinsic window-to-window scatter of $\sigma_\mathrm{int} = 0.127$ and 0.100 dex in the two ratios in quadrature with those uncertainties; it is the only contour directly comparable with the plotted points, which carry both terms, and it contains 14 of 29 (48%). The intrinsic scatter is thus a small fraction of a decade: the windows differ measurably in shape, but around one shape rather than across the plane.

Of 115 windows fitted, 113 returned a usable fit and 100 of those converged to a non-degenerate ellipsoid; axes are additionally clipped below 0.08, where the ratios are not trusted, leaving 94 drawn. Legend counts follow the drawing; the maximum-likelihood shape and the coverage fractions above use every usable window in the highest quartile (29). One highest-quartile window (r2800_c1200) falls below the axis floor and is not drawn; it is retained in the fit. 5 further windows from the lower tiers fall below the floor.
